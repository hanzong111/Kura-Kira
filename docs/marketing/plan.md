# Kura-Kira marketing plan

Draft, October 2026. Numbers marked *(target)* are goals to test, not data.

## 1. The idea in one line

**Kura-Kira is a cute pixel turtle that keeps a couple's money in their group chat.** You post what you spent,
the turtle counts it, and you both always know what's left and who owes whom.

The name does the work for us:

- **kura-kura** is Malay for turtle: slow, steady, patient, keeps everything safe in its shell.
- **kira** is Malay for count / calculate ("kira dulu" = work it out first).
- So Kura-Kira is *the turtle that counts*. The proverb fits too: **"Sikit-sikit, lama-lama jadi bukit"** (little by
  little, it becomes a hill).

## 2. Brand

### The mascot: Kura-Kira the turtle

- A small, round pixel-art turtle. Teal shell with cream rims, soft green skin, big friendly eyes.
- Wears a **two-tone scarf, coral and teal**: the two colours are the two partners (P1 coral, P2 teal), the same
  colours as the dashboard.
- Carries a tiny gold coin or a small abacus. Sometimes hides in its shell (privacy, "your money stays yours").
- Moods map to budget states, so the turtle can appear in the product later:
  happy (on track, green) · sweating (ahead of pace, yellow) · shell-hiding (over budget, red) · sleeping (nothing
  to answer) · counting coins (logging a spend) · question mark (asking who paid).

### Look

The same Co-op Arcade pixel style as the dashboard, so ads, posts and the product feel like one thing:

| Use | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Plum night | `#211B32` |
| Panels | Cream | `#F7EDCF` / `#FFF7E3` |
| P1 accent | Coral | `#F26F73` |
| P2 accent, turtle shell | Teal | `#62B6BB` |
| On track, turtle skin | Green | `#5F9E71` |
| Ahead of pace, coins | Yellow | `#F6CB45` |
| Over budget | Red | `#E85167` |

Headings in a chunky pixel font (Press Start 2P), body in a readable pixel font (VT323). Hard 4px shadows, square
corners, no gradients.

### Voice

Calm, warm, a little cheeky. Talks like a Malaysian friend who's good with money, never like a bank.

- **Do:** Manglish and Malay where natural ("gaji dah masuk!", "kira dulu"), short sentences, real ringgit amounts,
  jokes about kopi and Grab, celebrate small wins.
- **Don't:** shame people for spending, promise savings we can't prove ("save RM500 a month!"), give investment
  advice, sound like a fintech ad.
- **Never:** claim it's a bank, a financial adviser, or that the AI is perfect. Our honest claim is better: *the AI
  only reads the message; code does every calculation.*

### Name decision

The dashboard logo currently says **DUIT** (from the asset pack). Before launch, switch the product to one name:
**Kura-Kira**, with the turtle as the logo. Use "DUIT" nowhere public.

## 3. Who it's for

| Persona | Who | What they feel | What sells it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The new couple** | 23–30, dating 1–3 years, both working, often Grab-and-kopi spenders | "Who paid last time?" is awkward. Splitting feels unromantic. | No awkward maths, the turtle remembers. Cute enough to share. |
| **The engaged / newly married** | 26–35, saving for a wedding, house or car | Need a plan, don't want a spreadsheet or another app. | Weekly budgets with carry-over, house and car bills, month view. |
| **The living-together pair** | Renting together, sharing groceries, bills, the cat | Shared costs get messy, one person always pays more. | Who paid vs whose spend, balance, settle-up in one message. |

Start in Malaysia (Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru), English + Malay, with Chinese posts on Xiaohongshu.

## 4. Positioning and messages

**One-liner:** *Your money turtle. Post what you spend in your couple chat; Kura-Kira counts it.*

**Four message pillars:**

1. **No new app.** You already text each other. Just post `nasi lemak 12` and you're done.
2. **Fair, without the fights.** Who paid, who owes, split 50/50 or 2:1. The turtle keeps score so you don't have to.
3. **Every sen counted, correctly.** The AI only reads your message. Every total is calculated by plain code and
   checked against what you wrote.
4. **Actually cute.** A pixel dashboard you'll want to open, and screenshot.

**Objections and answers:**

| They say | We say |
|---|---|
| "We already use Splitwise / a spreadsheet." | Kura-Kira lives in your chat: no app to open, nothing to type twice. It does budgets too, not only splits. |
| "AI makes mistakes with numbers." | Right, so ours doesn't do the numbers. It only reads the message; code counts, and every amount must appear in what you typed. |
| "Is my data safe?" | Your ledger is private to your household. We never sell data. (Write the privacy policy before launch.) |
| "Will we keep using it?" | Weekly check-ins, payday reminders and a month-end report from the turtle. Logging takes five seconds. |

## 6. Launch plan

### Phase 0: get ready (weeks 1–2)

- [ ] Final name and turtle mascot (prompts in [image-prompts.md](image-prompts.md)); swap the dashboard logo.
- [ ] One-page website: hero, how it works in 3 steps, the dashboard screenshot, pricing, waitlist form.
- [ ] Social handles: `@kurakira` (or closest) on TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Xiaohongshu; a Telegram channel.
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms (PDPA), "not financial advice" line.
- [ ] Make 12 posts and 6 short videos before announcing anything (see section 7).
- [ ] Founder story: how two people built a turtle to stop arguing about who paid for kopi.

### Phase 1: private beta (weeks 3–6)

- 20 couples *(target)* from friends, colleagues and the waitlist, set up by hand, on the founding price.
- A shared Telegram group for beta feedback; fix the top 3 complaints every week.
- Collect 5 quotes and 3 before/after stories ("we stopped arguing about who paid").
- Measure: did they log something in the first 24 hours? Are they still logging in week 4?

### Phase 2: public launch (weeks 7–10)

- Launch week: founder story video, "Meet Kura-Kira" post, carousel "how it works", live demo on TikTok.
- Time it near **payday (25th to 1st)**: "Gaji dah masuk. Kira dulu, baru belanja."
- Gift a free year to 10 micro-creators *(target)* who post about couples, money or Malaysian food (10k–50k
  followers do better than big accounts here).
- Founding-couple price ends when 100 couples have joined, or at the end of launch month.

### Phase 3: grow (week 11 on)

- **Referrals:** "Bring a couple": both couples get a free month.
- **Challenges:** "30-day Kira challenge": log every spend for 30 days, share your month card, win a free year.
- **Partners:** wedding planners and bridal fairs (a free 3 months in every wedding package), couple
  photographers, financial-literacy creators.
- **WhatsApp version**, since most Malaysian couples live there.

## 7. Content plan

Post 4–5 times a week: 3 short videos, 1–2 images or carousels. Same turtle, same palette, every time.

| Pillar | Share | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| **Couple money moments** (relatable, funny) | 35% | "Who paid for dinner? 🐢 knows." · "Me: one kopi only. Also me: kopi #6." · "When your partner says 'I'll pay you back' (3 months ago)." |
| **How it works** (product) | 25% | 15-second screen recording: type `zapfan 27.70 split` → turtle replies. The weekly carry-over explained with coins. The dashboard tour. |
| **Money tips for two** (useful, never preachy) | 20% | "The 20/100 rule: RM20 a weekday, RM100 a weekend day." "3 money talks every couple should have." "How to split rent fairly when salaries are different." |

## 9. What to measure

| Metric | Why | Target *(first 3 months)* |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist → setup | Is the page convincing? | 40% |
| **Activation:** first spend logged within 24 hours of setup | Did they get it? | 70% |
| **Week-4 retention:** still logging in week 4 | The one number that says "product works" | 50% |
| Trial → paid | Is it worth RM12.90? | 25% |
| Referrals per paying couple | Will it spread? | 0.3 |
| Cost per paying couple from ads | Can we buy growth? | under RM40 |

Check them every Monday. If week-4 retention is low, fix the product before spending more on marketing.

## 10. Risks and rules

- **Claims:** no "save RMx" promises without data; no investment advice. Every page carries "not financial advice".
- **Data:** a PDPA-compliant privacy policy, data export and delete, before taking money.
- **Platforms:** don't use Telegram's or WhatsApp's logos in ads. In-bot sales of digital goods on Telegram must use
  Telegram Stars; sell on the website instead.
- **Real people:** Hanz and Vanisa's pixel characters are personal. Use them in the founder story only if you're
  both happy to; ads use the turtle and a generic pixel couple.
- **Trust moments:** if the bot gets an amount wrong for a beta couple, fix it fast and say so publicly. Being honest
  about mistakes helps our "every sen counted" message.
